to protect the heart.

Our programs target mechanisms associated with the fibro-fatty remodeling seen in arrhythmogenic cardiomyopathy. Both programs are in research and development; stage details were not provided in the source materials.

PROGRAM
01
RESEARCH & DEVELOPMENTEPIGENETICS

HDAC3 inhibitors

Targeting the fibro-fatty substrate in genetic cardiomyopathy

Kardyon’s proprietary HDAC3 inhibitors are designed to target epigenetic regulators associated with pathological remodeling. The research aims to shift cardiac gene expression away from degeneration and toward functional recovery, with the goal of protecting the structural integrity of the heart.

HDAC3
PROGRAM
02
RESEARCH & DEVELOPMENTCARDIAC SIGNALING

BMP10

Harnessing a heart-specific signaling pathway

BMP10 is a heart-specific member of the TGF-beta superfamily. Kardyon’s research investigates its potential to counter pro-fibrotic and pro-adipogenic programs associated with ACM. The program also explores BMP10-associated SMAD1/5/8 signaling and a non-canonical STAT3 pathway in cardiac repair.
